# Product Marketing Context — ScanForProfit

*Last updated: June 2026*

## Product Overview

**One-liner:** ScanForProfit is the sourcing and tracking app for eBay resellers — scan any item or a full shelf, get an instant HOT, LIST, or SKIP decision, and run your whole business from your browser.

**What it does:** ScanForProfit lets resellers photograph any item at a thrift store, garage sale, or estate sale and get an AI-powered sourcing decision with real profit math in seconds. It also scans entire shelves at once, ranks every visible item by profit potential (HOT → LIST → SKIP), and feeds sourcing decisions directly into a built-in inventory tracker, P&L, and growth insights tool. Everything a reseller needs — sourcing, inventory, listing, tracking, trends — in one app.

**Product category:** Reseller sourcing app / thrift store scanner app (how customers search: "thrift store scanner app," "eBay reseller app," "reseller profit calculator," "thrift store price checker")

**Product type:** Web app at scanforprofit.com/app.html — works from any browser, no install required. Mobile app is roadmap.

**Business model:** Freemium SaaS
- **Trial** — Free for 7 days: unlimited scans and inventory
- **Scout** — Free forever: 25 AI scans/month, 10 inventory items, listing generator, eBay export, profit calculator
- **Hustle** — $19/mo: 250 scans/month, 250 inventory items, full listing generator, P&L dashboard, photo enhancement
- **Stack** — $49/mo: Unlimited scans, unlimited inventory, growth agent, all features
- **Empire** — $199/mo: Up to 10 seats, team features

*Free tier available — no credit card required.*

## Target Audience

**Who they are:** Solo resellers and small reselling operations (1–3 people) who source from thrift stores, estate sales, garage sales, and flea markets and sell primarily on eBay.

**Volume:** Part-time (20–50 items/month) to full-time (200+ items/month).

**Current tools they use:** eBay Seller Hub, Google Sheets, a notes app, maybe a random scanner app they read about in a Facebook group. Nothing talks to anything else.

**Primary use case:** Making fast, confident sourcing decisions at the thrift store without spending 10 minutes manually Googling every item.

**Jobs to be done:**
1. "Tell me if this is worth buying — right now, while I'm standing in the store"
2. "Show me what everything on this shelf is worth so I don't miss anything"
3. "Help me know what I actually made this month — not a rough idea, a real number"
4. "Tell me what to go hunt for this week based on what's actually selling"

**Specific scenarios:**
- Standing in Goodwill with 45 seconds before someone else grabs the item
- Walking an estate sale and needing to triage 200 items in 90 minutes
- Getting home and not knowing if the pile on the floor will make money
- Realizing at tax time that they have no idea what their actual expenses were

## Problems & Pain Points

**Core problem:** Resellers make or lose money at the moment of sourcing — but they have no reliable, fast way to know if something is worth buying while they're standing in the store. Manual eBay research takes 2–5 minutes per item. At a thrift store with 200 items, that math doesn't work.

**Why current solutions fall short:**
- **eBay app barcode scanner** — only works on items with barcodes. Misses everything valuable: vintage clothing, ceramics, electronics without barcodes, handmade items
- **Google Lens** — identifies items but gives no pricing, no profit math, no decision
- **Underpriced.ai** — single item only, no shelf scan, no inventory tracking, no P&L, no growth insights. Requires its own subscription
- **Manual eBay sold search** — takes 2–5 min per item, requires mental math on fees, still no inventory integration
- **Spreadsheets** — manual data entry, no AI, abandoned after a few weeks

**What it costs them:**
- Time: 2–5 minutes per item × dozens of items per sourcing trip = hours lost
- Money: Bad buys from guessing — items that sit unsold for months, capital tied up
- Mental load: 47 unlisted items in a pile, no idea what they're worth

**Emotional tension:**
- Anxiety of not knowing if something is worth buying while someone else is eyeing it
- Frustration of getting home and realizing they overpaid
- Overwhelm of running a business on spreadsheets and guesswork
- The embarrassment of not knowing their own profit margin

## Competitive Landscape

**Direct competitors:**
- **Underpriced.ai** ($12/mo, 30 scans) — Photo-based AI sourcing tool, pulls real eBay sold comps, calculates profit after fees. Falls short: single items only (no shelf scan), no inventory system, no P&L, no growth insights, requires separate subscription
- **ThriftMagic** — Has a bookshelf scanner but reviews cite it as slow (5+ min to process) and unreliable. Falls short: focused on books/media only, inconsistent results, not built for mixed-category resellers
- **Curio** — Antiques/collectibles specialist. Falls short: too narrow, not for general thrift resellers

**Secondary competitors (same problem, different solution):**
- Manual eBay sold listings search — free but slow, no profit math, no inventory integration
- Google Lens — free identification but zero pricing data

**Indirect competitors (doing nothing / current workflow):**
- The "gut feel + hope" approach — resellers who just guess based on experience
- The spreadsheet stack — eBay Seller Hub + Google Sheets + notes app + random scanner

**The real competition is the habit of doing it manually.** Most resellers don't switch tools because what they do now "works" — they just don't know how much they're leaving on the table.

## Differentiation

**Key differentiators — what nothing else does:**
1. **Shelf scan** — one wide photo of a full shelf, every item ranked by profit potential. Unique in the market.
2. **Integrated system** — sourcing decision → inventory → listing → P&L → growth insights, all in one app. Competitors solve one piece; ScanForProfit solves the whole workflow.
3. **Configurable fee structure** — profit math uses *your* actual eBay fee rate (8–18% slider), not a hardcoded assumption. No other tool does this.
4. **Growth agent** — AI analyzes your inventory against live eBay trends and tells you what to hunt for this week. Proactive, not just reactive.

**How we do it differently:** AI does the identification, pricing research, profit math, and decision — all from a photo. User just takes a picture and reads the answer.

**Why that's better:** Seconds vs. 2–5 minutes per item. Whole shelf vs. one item. One app vs. four apps and two spreadsheets.

**Why customers choose ScanForProfit:**
- Speed at the shelf — fastest decision possible
- Shelf scan surfaces profitable items they would have walked past
- Everything integrated means nothing falls through the cracks
- They finally know their real profit numbers

**Key conversion actions:**
1. New user: complete first scan (aha moment)

**North Star metric:** Scans completed per user per week (indicates active sourcing use, correlates with retention and paid conversion).
