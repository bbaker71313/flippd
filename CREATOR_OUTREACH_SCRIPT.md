# FLIPPD — Creator Outreach Script & Target List

**Goal:** Get 3-5 reselling creators to try Flippd. Organic promotion + testimonial opportunities.

---

## TARGET CREATOR PROFILE

**Ideal targets:**
- 10k-100k followers (high engagement, reachable)
- Content: Thrift hauls, reselling how-tos, profit breakdowns
- Activity: Posted in last 2 weeks (active)
- Platforms: TikTok, YouTube, Instagram

**Why this range:**
- <10k: No reach yet
- 10k-100k: Perfect sweet spot (credible + accessible)
- >100k: Harder to reach, may want sponsorship fees

---

## RESPONSE TYPES & HOW TO HANDLE

### Response: "This looks cool, I'll try it"

Reply:
```
Awesome! Here's your code: [CODE]

Go to flippd.com, enter the code. Full access to everything for early access period.

Let me know what you find. Seriously — I read every reply.

— Britt
```

Then:
- Give them code
- Ask them to send you videos/screenshots if they use it
- Don't ask for testimonial yet (let them organically decide to promote)

---

### Response: "How much do you pay creators?"

Reply:
```
Right now, no money — just early access + the chance to be one of the first to use something new.

If it's useful and you want to make a video about it, that's awesome. If not, no pressure.

Just trying to get feedback from people in the reselling world.

— Britt
```

Then:
- Only offer payment if they ask again
- Don't pay for testimonials (violates FTC rules + comes across as fake)
- Authentic organic endorsement >>> paid sponsored content

---

### Response: "Not interested"

Reply:
```
Fair! Always worth asking though.

If anything changes (you start sourcing differently, reselling becomes priority), I'm here.

Good luck with your content!

— Britt
```

Then:
- Don't follow up again
- Move to next creator

---

### Response: "This is awesome, I want to make a video"

Reply:
```
YES. Please. Here's your code: [CODE]

Two asks:
1. When you make the video, tag me @flippd or @britt so I can share it
2. Let me know before you post so I can prepare an email blast to our early access users (more views = more comments = better for you)

No script. Just be honest about what you use and what works.

— Britt
```

Then:
- Ask if they want to be quoted/featured on landing page
- Send them the landing page after they post so they see it live
- Share their video to all your channels + email list
