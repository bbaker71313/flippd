# FLIPPD — Social Content Calendar (Early Access Phase)

**30 posts across 4 weeks. TikTok + Instagram. Post 1-2x daily.**

**Content Pillars:**
- The Find (40%): Thrift haul + Flippd result + profit breakdown
- The Win (25%): User success story or profitable flip
- The Mistake (20%): "I almost overpaid — here's why Flippd saved me $X"
- The Culture (15%): Behind-the-scenes, team building energy

**Production Notes:**
- iPhone footage is fine. No fancy production.
- Vertical video (9:16 for TikTok, 4:5 for Instagram).
- Light text overlay OK. Authentic beats polished.
- Post same video to both TikTok + Instagram (slight text adjustments).

## POSTING SCHEDULE

**Week 1:** Daily (start Monday)
**Week 2-4:** 1-2 posts per day (Monday-Saturday, rest Sunday for planning)

**Best Times:**
- TikTok: 6-9 AM, 12-2 PM, 7-10 PM (resellers checking before/after work)
- Instagram: Same times + evening (7-11 PM)
- Stagger posts 4-6 hours apart to avoid algorithm saturation

---

## METRICS TO TRACK

- Views (target: 1k+/post by week 3)
- Engagement rate (target: 5%+)
- Shares (goal: increase each week)
- Click-throughs to landing page (track via UTM: utm_source=instagram&utm_medium=social)
- Email signups from social links (target: 10% of visitors)

---

## COPY NOTES

- Every post has one clear CTA or insight
- No sales pitch until the ask at the end ("Want in? Link in bio.")
- Show the BENEFIT first (profit, time saved), then mention Flippd
- Authentic > polished
- Use "I" and "you," not "we" and "customers"
- Reseller language throughout (flip, profit, comps, sourcing trip, thrift store)

---

## OWNERSHIP & UPDATES

**Owner:** Social/Content  
**Frequency:** Update after each 4-week cycle based on engagement data  
**Testing:** A/B subject matter (more mistakes vs. more wins) — track engagement
