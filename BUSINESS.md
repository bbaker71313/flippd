# Flippd — Business Plan

The full picture: who we are, who we serve, how we make money, how we grow, and where this is going.

---

## The One-Sentence Version

Flippd is the AI-powered sourcing and business tool for solo resellers — scan any item or a whole shelf at a thrift store, get an instant FLIP or PASS with real profit math, and run your entire reselling business from your phone.

---

## The Problem We Solve

Resellers make or lose money at the moment of sourcing. Standing in a Goodwill with 45 seconds before someone else grabs an item, they need to know: is this worth buying? What will it sell for? What's my profit after eBay fees?

Manual eBay research takes 2–5 minutes per item. At a thrift store with 200 items on a shelf, that math doesn't work.

The tools that exist today each solve a piece of this:
- **eBay's barcode scanner** — only works on items with barcodes. Most valuable thrift finds don't have barcodes.
- **Google Lens** — identifies items but gives zero pricing or profit data.
- **Underpriced.ai** ($12/mo) — AI photo scan with profit math. Single items only. No shelf scan. No inventory. No P&L.
- **Spreadsheets** — manual, abandoned after a few weeks.

**The gap:** No tool does whole-shelf scanning, integrates sourcing with inventory tracking, and handles the full business workflow in one app. Flippd does.

## Who We Serve

**Primary:** Solo eBay resellers sourcing from thrift stores, estate sales, garage sales, and flea markets. Mixed-category (electronics, clothing, home goods, collectibles). Part-time (20–50 items/month) to full-time (200+ items/month).

**Secondary:** Small reselling operations (2–3 people) who want shared inventory and a team dashboard.

**Not our customer:**
- Amazon FBA sellers (they need Jungle Scout/Helium 10)
- Resellers who only sell new-in-box items with barcodes
- Enterprise warehouse operations

**Where they live online:**
- r/Flipping, r/Ebay (Reddit)
- Facebook reseller groups (Resellers Anonymous, etc.)
- TikTok (#thriftflip, #reseller, #ebayseller)
- YouTube (reselling vlog communities)

---

## The Product

**Core loop:** Scan → Decide → Add to Inventory → List → Track → Repeat

**What makes Flippd different:**

1. **Shelf scan** — One wide photo of a full shelf. Every visible item ranked HOT / FLIP / PASS by profit potential. Nothing else in the market does this.

2. **Integrated workflow** — Sourcing decision flows directly into inventory. Inventory feeds the P&L. The P&L informs what to hunt next. One app, no copy-pasting between tools.

3. **Configurable fee math** — Uses your actual eBay fee rate (not a hardcoded assumption). The profit calculation is yours, not a generic estimate.

4. **Market intelligence** — Pulls live eBay trends and tells you what's selling this week, what's stale in your inventory, and what to hunt for. Proactive, not just reactive.

**Hustle — $19/mo ($15 annual)**

**Stack — $49/mo ($39 annual)**
- Everything in Hustle
- Unlimited inventory

**Empire — $199/mo (up to 10 seats)**
- Everything in Stack
- Team seats + permissions
- Shared inventory
- Manager dashboard
- API access
- Priority support

### Unit Economics

- **API cost per scan:** ~$0.02–$0.05 (Claude API, image + text)
- **Hustle user margin:** ~$19 revenue – ~$1–2 API cost (50 scans/mo avg) = ~$17 gross margin per user
- **Stack user margin:** ~$49 revenue – ~$3–5 API cost = ~$44 gross margin per user
- **Gross margin:** ~85–90% at scale (very strong for SaaS)

### Value Metric

**Scans per month** — the natural gate. A reseller who does 2 sourcing trips a week scanning 20 items each = 160 scans/month. Free tier runs out after 25. That's the upgrade moment.

### Pricing rationale

- $19/mo is less than one good flip. A reseller who finds one $50 profit item per month using Flippd has already paid for Hustle 2.6x over.
- $49/mo targets full-time resellers doing 100+ items/month. One good week pays for the year.
- Underpriced.ai charges $12/mo for 30 scans and doesn't have shelf scan or inventory. We're priced above them on features, not just price.

## Go-To-Market Strategy

### Phase 1: Internal (Now)
**Goal:** Prove the app works for a real reseller (Britt).
**Status:** Complete. App is tested and working.

### Phase 2: Early Access (Next 2–4 weeks)
**Goal:** 25–50 real resellers using the app. First paying users.

**Channel 1 — Owned: Email list**
The landing page captures emails. Every other channel feeds this list. This is the asset we build.
- Rewrite landing page with honest copy (no fake metrics/testimonials)
- Email capture with clear value prop: "Get early access to the shelf-scanning app for eBay resellers"
- Welcome sequence: what Flippd does, how shelf scan works, how to use it at your next thrift trip

**Channel 2 — Rented: TikTok + Instagram**
Resellers are already making content. The format is perfect:
- Film a sourcing trip using Flippd. Show the scan result. Show the profit.
- "I found this at Goodwill for $4. Flippd said FLIP. It sold for $67. Here's the math."
- No production value needed. Authentic beats polished for this audience.
- Every post drives to the email list / landing page.

**Channel 3 — Borrowed: Reseller community outreach**
- Post genuinely useful content in r/Flipping and r/Ebay (not spam — answer questions, provide value)
- Reach out to 5–10 reselling YouTubers/TikTokers with a free access code
- Join Facebook reseller groups, be helpful, introduce the tool naturally
- Find reselling newsletter writers (there are several with 5k–50k subscribers)

### Phase 3: Beta Launch (Weeks 4–8)
**Goal:** 100–250 users, 20–50 paying. Validate conversion rate.

**Add paid channel:** Once there's social proof and testimonials, test Meta ads targeting:
- Interest: eBay selling, thrift shopping, reselling
- Lookalike: email list
- Budget: $10–20/day to start. Watch cost-per-signup.

**Product Hunt:** Prepare a listing. Launch when the proxy is live and the app is polished. Product Hunt works for reaching early adopters — resellers are a subset of this.

**Reseller podcasts:** There are 10+ active reselling podcasts. Pitch as a guest. "I built an app that scans an entire shelf and tells you what to buy" is a compelling hook.

### Phase 4: Full Launch (Month 3–4)
**Goal:** Open self-serve signups. Start charging everyone.

**Open the free tier.** Anyone can sign up and get 25 scans. No invite needed.

**Convert waitlist.** Email everyone who signed up during early access. First 500 get 50% off forever (if this program is actually being run — do not offer this unless it's real).

**PR push.** Pitch to:
- Reselling-specific media (Reseller's Mindset, FlipIt, etc.)
- General entrepreneurship/side hustle media (Side Hustle Nation, The Hustle)
- Local news angle: "Local reseller builds AI app that's changing how people thrift"

## Retention Strategy

### Activation (first 7 days)
The aha moment is the first scan result. Get users to that moment as fast as possible.
- Welcome email: "Here's how to use Flippd on your next thrift trip" (practical, not generic)
- Day 3: "Did you try the shelf scan?" — most users don't find this feature without prompting
- Day 7: "What did you flip this week?" — drives second sourcing trip

### Retention (ongoing)
- Weekly "hunt list" email: what to look for this week based on eBay trends
- "You have X stale items" notification — creates urgency to re-engage
- Monthly P&L summary: "Here's what you made last month with Flippd"

### Churn prevention
When users hit cancel:
- "Too expensive" → offer 25% off for 3 months
- "Not using it enough" → offer 1-month pause
- "Missing a feature" → show roadmap, ask what they need

### Win-back
- 30 days after churn: "What would bring you back? Here's what's new."
- 90 days after churn: "Hustle has gotten better. Here's a free month."
