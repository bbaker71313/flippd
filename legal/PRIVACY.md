# Privacy Policy

**Flippd takes your privacy seriously.** This policy explains how we handle your data.

## Data Storage

### What We Store Locally (On Your Device)

Your Flippd data lives **only in your browser** using localStorage:
- ✅ Inventory items (photos, cost, sell price, status)
- ✅ Settings (eBay fee %, packaging cost)
- ✅ Scan history
- ✅ P&L calculations
- ✅ All photos you upload

**We cannot access this data.** It never leaves your device unless you choose to export it.

### What We Send to Our Servers

Very little:
- ✅ Photos you submit for scanning (analyzed by AI, then deleted)
- ✅ Email address (if you sign up for early access)
- ✅ Basic analytics (page views, button clicks)

**We do NOT send:**
- ❌ Your inventory data
- ❌ Your saved photos
- ❌ Your cost/profit data
- ❌ Any personally identifiable information beyond email

## How Photos Are Used

1. You take a photo in Flippd
2. Photo is sent to our AI service (Claude API via Anthropic)
3. AI identifies the item, looks up comps, calculates profit
4. Result is returned to your device
5. **Photo is immediately deleted from our servers** (not stored)

**We do not:**
- ❌ Store photos
- ❌ Use photos to train models
- ❌ Share photos with third parties
- ❌ Sell or monetize photos

## Data Security

### In Transit
- All data is encrypted (HTTPS/SSL)
- No unencrypted transmission

### At Rest
- Photos are processed but not stored
- Email list stored in secure email provider (Mailchimp, ConvertKit, etc.)
- No user data stored in our database (it's all local to you)

### Anthropic API
- Flippd sends photos to Anthropic's Claude API
- Anthropic's privacy policy applies: https://www.anthropic.com/privacy
- Photos are not used for model training (Claude API data policy)

## Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand how Flippd is used:
- Page views (which pages are visited)
- User engagement (scroll depth, time on page)
- Conversion events (email signups)

**We do NOT track:**
- ❌ Individual user identities
- ❌ Your inventory data
- ❌ Your location

You can opt out of analytics in your browser settings.

## Email Communications

If you sign up for early access:
- ✅ We send you your access code
- ✅ We send you updates about new features
- ✅ You can unsubscribe anytime
- ❌ We never sell your email to third parties

## Data Deletion

**Your data is always yours:**

### Delete Locally
Clear your browser's localStorage:
1. Right-click in Flippd
2. Inspect → Application → Local Storage → flippd.com → Clear All
3. All your data is gone (cannot be recovered)

### Delete from Email List
1. Click "Unsubscribe" in any email
2. Your email is removed from our list immediately
3. We no longer contact you

### Request Data Export
1. Reply to support@flippd.com with "I want my data export"
2. We provide your email + any data we have (very minimal)
3. No questions asked

Each service has its own privacy policy. Check before using.

## Children's Privacy

Flippd is not intended for children under 13. If you're under 13, please don't use Flippd.

If we become aware a child under 13 has signed up, we'll delete their data immediately.
